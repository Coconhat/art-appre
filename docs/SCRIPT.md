# Script: Gaano Kalayo? (How far is far?)

Short lines to say, one part at a time. Each part has **Say** (what happens), **Philippines** and
**World** (why it matters). The numbers are the ones on screen; their sources are on `/statement#sources`.

**Open with:** "We did not start with a slogan. We started with a question: how far is far?"

---

## 1. Simula (Beginning)
- **Say:** A question, nothing else. Not yet where, not yet who.
- **Philippines:** We hear "far away" about Palestine all the time.
- **World:** Distance is how people look away. The piece begins by taking it apart.

## 2. Tahanan (Home)
- **Say:** Think of your home. Someone is at the door. You have one hour. What do you carry?
- **Philippines:** Every Filipino knows what home means, and what it is to be apart from it.
- **World:** In 1948 hundreds of thousands of Palestinians lost their homes, and many still cannot go back. This is how it feels.

## 3. Susi (The key)
- **Say:** Most people take the key. In 1948, about 750,000 Palestinians did. Their families still keep them.
- **Philippines:** A key means "I will be back", something we understand.
- **World:** The Nakba. Nearly 6 million Palestine refugees today. Palestine is named here for the first time.

## 4. Boto (The vote)
- **Say:** 29 November 1947. The UN votes to divide Palestine. The Philippines votes yes, 33 to 13.
- **Philippines:** Our delegate, Romulo, spoke against the plan three days earlier, and we still voted yes. We are in this story.
- **World:** A vote on someone else's land, where the people living there were not asked.

## 5. Pader (The wall)
- **Say:** 109 years of history on concrete, 1917 to 2026, including 7 October 2023.
- **Philippines:** We know what it is to have others draw our maps.
- **World:** The history did not start in 2023. This shows both sides of it.

## 6. Lakad (The walk)
- **Say:** Walk the Gaza Strip. You can steer, you cannot go back, and every path ends at the wall at Rafah.
- **Philippines:** Gaza is 41 km long, about twice the size of Quezon City, with 2.1 million people.
- **World:** A small strip with nowhere to go. You feel the trap by walking it.

## 7. Bilang (The count)
- **Say:** One light for every person killed: 74,561 (as of 27 October 2026). The small ones are children, more than 17,000.
- **Philippines:** Picture a town, a barangay, your street.
- **World:** The real number is likely higher. A study in The Lancet estimated about 41% more. Lighting one candle a second takes 20 hours 43 minutes.

## 8. Sining (The art)
- **Say:** What people make when everything is taken: tatreez, Handala, the watermelon, the kite poem.
- **Philippines:** Our own art, Luna's Spoliarium, bore witness to our colonization too.
- **World:** Art is how a people says "we were here, and we are still here." These are the real works.

## 9. Sinulid (The thread)
- **Say:** 8,843 km from Manila to Gaza, sewn shut with red thread.
- **Philippines:** About 30,000 Filipinos work in Israel. Four were killed on 7 October 2023. 17 Filipino sailors were held on the Galaxy Leader for 429 days.
- **World:** Wars pull ordinary workers in. And the Philippines has the sun on its flag, and tatreez has the star.
- **Line to land:** *Hindi malayo.* It is not far.

## 10. Halaga (The price)
- **Say:** *Halaga* means both price and worth. The war spread: the Red Sea, then Hormuz.
- **Philippines:** Diesel went from ₱56.50 to ₱153.70 a litre. Inflation went from 2.4% to 7.2%. 1.34 million Filipinos at risk of poverty. About 98% of our crude oil comes from the Middle East, and 2.4 million Filipinos work there.
- **World:** Suez Canal income fell from $9.4 billion to $7.2 billion. Oil went from about $72 to $118 a barrel. Rebuilding Gaza alone: about $71.4 billion.
- **Line to land:** "We pay in pesos. Gaza pays in lives."

## 11. Kalayaan (Freedom)
- **Say:** The flag's colours come back, together, for the first time. "Malayang Palestina. فلسطين حرة. Free Palestine."
- **Philippines:** Kalayaan is our word for freedom too. We know it cost us.
- **World:** Learn. Give. Speak. Remember. Fly a kite. The child has still not turned around.

---

**Close with:** "How far is far? Not far at all. Hindi malayo."
